Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Przyjazne Emocje (the “App”), developed and provided by Instytut Wspomagania Rozwoju Dziecka, Poland (“we”, “us”).

2. About the application

Przyjazne Emocje is an educational application designed to support children in recognizing and naming basic emotions based on pictures or icons displayed on the screen. The app provides interactive exercises where the child identifies the correct emotion when prompted.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect any data. The App has no user accounts, no backend server, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Content you create in the App, such as photos, materials, and settings, is stored only on your device. We have no access to it.

4. Device access and permissions

When creating learning materials, you can optionally take a photo with the camera or choose an image from your device. The App also uses the device’s text-to-speech feature to read content aloud. Nothing is transmitted outside the device.

5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not share any personal data with third parties, as the App does not collect personal data.

6. Data retention

All content is stored locally and is removed when you uninstall the App. Older versions of the App may store photos and settings in a folder in the device’s shared storage; these files remain on the device until you delete them. If Android backup is enabled on your device, App data may be included in your personal Google backup, which is managed by Google under your account, not by us.

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

We do not process any personal data, as all content stays on the user’s device. Therefore no legal basis for data processing under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) is required.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is designed for children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
